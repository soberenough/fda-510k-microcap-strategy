# Multi-AI Discussion Prompt

Use this prompt (or a variation) when inviting Claude, ChatGPT, or other models to review the topic.

---

**Context**: We are evaluating a potential trading idea around FDA 510(k) medical device clearances for microcap public companies (trading under $5).

**Premise**: The FDA updates its public 510(k) database. Small firms may lag 12–24 hours on press releases. Buy the stock on the database update, sell when the PR hits for a large gain.

**Known facts so far**:
- FDA states the 510(k) database is updated once a week.
- Bulk files monthly.
- Company receives the letter before the public database entry.
- Many services already watch this space.

**Tasks for reviewers**:
1. Critique the premise rigorously. Is there a real information edge?
2. Suggest methods to quantify historical lags and price reactions (without providing trading signals).
3. Identify practical barriers (liquidity, name-to-ticker matching, materiality).
4. Propose what data or analysis would be needed to decide if this is worth further investigation.
5. Flag any regulatory or practical risks.

Reference the files in this repo. Be skeptical and evidence-based. Not investment advice.
