# FDA 510(k) Microcap Strategy Research

Collaborative research repo for evaluating whether monitoring the FDA 510(k) database can provide a trading edge in microcap (<$5) medical device / biotech stocks.

**Core idea under review**: FDA updates the official public 510(k) clearance ledger. Small companies may take 12–24 hours to issue a press release. Can one buy before the PR and sell for large gains?

## Status

Early-stage research. Initial analysis suggests the premise has significant flaws (database not real-time, competition, liquidity issues), but isolated opportunistic trades may be possible. This repo is for multi-AI discussion, historical review, data collection, and rigorous evaluation of whether it is worth pursuing.

## Key Questions

1. What is the actual update frequency of the public 510(k) database vs. when companies receive the letter?
2. How often do public microcaps have a meaningful lag between clearance letter and widely disseminated PR/8-K?
3. What has the historical price reaction been for microcap 510(k) clearances?
4. How material is a typical 510(k) for a sub-$5 stock?
5. Liquidity, spreads, and practical execution constraints?
6. Is there a systematic edge after accounting for competition and costs?

## Repo Structure
