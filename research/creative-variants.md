# Creative / Unconventional Variants (Grok)

The narrow original idea (buy the microcap stock on the weekly FDA DB entry before the PR, sell for +50%) faces hard constraints. These variants keep the core signal (public 510(k) clearance for small medtech) but change instrument, timing, or structure.

## 1. Literal options
- Many sub-$5 medtech names have listed calls/puts (or can be approximated).
- A 30-80% stock move can turn cheap short-dated OTM calls into multi-baggers.
- Test only names with sufficient options liquidity; size for 100% loss.
- Also consider LEAPs or calendar spreads if the clearance is expected but timing is uncertain.

## 2. Pre-clearance positioning
- Companies often guide on expected 510(k) timelines in filings or calls.
- Small defined-risk position ahead of a high-probability decision window.
- Distinct from pure post-publication DB edge; higher information risk.

## 3. Peer / basket / competitive
- Clearance in a product code can lift (or pressure) other public companies in the same space.
- Trade higher-liquidity peers or a small basket instead of the thin microcap.
- Or short a competitor if the device is truly disruptive.

## 4. Financing & capital structure
- Small medtech frequently raise (shelf, ATM, PIPE, warrant exercise) around or after clearance.
- The clearance + financing combination creates volatility in both directions.
- Monitor for the dual event.

## 5. Volatility / non-directional
- Long straddles/strangles where options exist (direction uncertain because some clearances disappoint).
- Or fade the initial spike if data shows mean-reversion.

## 6. Monitoring creativity
- Maintain a living map of public microcap applicants → tickers.
- Alert the moment the weekly FDA file updates; cross-check social/news for true first public mention.
- Combine with volume/price confirmation before entry.

## Research note
These are hypotheses for the event study, not trade recommendations. Any variant still needs point-in-time public data, realistic fills, and out-of-sample testing. The pure stock +50% version is the most constrained; the options and peer versions may have better asymmetry.
