# Initial Analysis (Grok, Nov 2025)

## Database Update Reality

- FDA’s Medical Device Databases page states the 510(k) database **is updated once a week**.
- Downloadable bulk files are typically replaced monthly (usually around the 5th).
- openFDA updates are monthly and can lag.
- The company receives the official clearance letter first. The public searchable database appears later.

There is no reliable “instant official public ledger” that precedes company announcements by 12–24 hours.

## Practical Observations

- Public companies generally announce promptly once they have the letter (material information).
- Specialized services and traders already monitor both company wires and FDA sources.
- Matching applicant names in the FDA database to public tickers is non-trivial.
- Not every 510(k) is material or a positive surprise.
- Microcap liquidity (wide spreads, thin volume) is a major execution risk.

## Historical Context

Examples of microcap moves on 510(k) news exist (double-digit to triple-digit percentage moves in some cases), but these are typically after the PR is issued, not from a pure database lag. Reactions are inconsistent; some stocks fall on the news if the clearance is narrower than expected.

## Preliminary Conclusion

Isolated opportunistic trades may be possible when there is a genuine lag and the clearance is material. A systematic “buy before the news, sell for 50%” strategy is not supported by the update cadence or practical constraints. Further historical data collection and multi-AI review recommended.
